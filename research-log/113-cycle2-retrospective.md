## Domain lessons

1. **The bottleneck was evidence availability, not a shortage of control
   ideas.** Current work already contains external release gates, anytime-valid
   commit tests, immutable evaluators, certificates, and constraint-plus-utility
   optimization. The missing artifact is complete candidate-level evidence.

2. **A correct abstract theorem is not an executable systems result.** OMST's
   factorization theorem and exact witness survived review, but framework/output
   correspondence still required an external trust root, complete dependency
   closure, authentic output origin, and atomic pair evidence.

3. **Constructed schedules cannot establish empirical prevalence.** PQF's
   outcome floor and clean guardrails were analytically forced by the authored
   schedule. Adding cases would have increased precision around a predetermined
   result, not supplied evidence.

4. **Whole-trace corpora are not interchangeable runtime telemetry.** The
   label-blind TBEA pilot achieved perfect primary coder agreement, yet effective
   termination authority was jointly recoverable in AppWorld 5/6, AG2 2/6, and
   MetaGPT 0/6. Missing telemetry cannot be converted into misalignment.

5. **Negative feasibility gates saved more budget than additional reviews would
   have.** TBEA stopped before label association, and IPHE stopped before a
   hypothesis or review dispatch. Both produced sharper reusable requirements.

6. **Competition performance needs competition feedback.** The prior public
   score of 69.570 and mock/live forecast failures show that synthetic mechanics
   cannot select a leaderboard winner. The next cycle must recover evaluator
   state and measured target behavior before inventing another optimization.

## Evidence mined from the cycle

### Learnings

- The state carries promoted lessons on constructed-to-live non-transfer
  (`recurrences=3`), replay-tail calibration (`3`), and machine-readable
  protocol underdetermination (`7`). All three apply directly to competition
  work: local success is not leaderboard confidence, replay cost needs a tail
  model, and the submission contract must be mechanically complete.
- New Cycle-2 lessons identify self-hashed-manifest authenticity, output-origin
  closure, and candidate-lineage identifiability as distinct from internal
  consistency.

### Tried and failed

Four Cycle-2 approaches closed with different root causes:

| Approach | Failure class in state | Root cause |
|---|---|---|
| OMST authenticated correspondence | `implementation_defeated` | External trust and complete runtime/output authentication were absent. |
| PQF v2 synthetic census | `design_defeated` | The author-controlled schedule forced the claimed floor and defeated anti-stacking. |
| TBEA on MAST | `measurement_unidentifiable` | Only one system met the frozen authority-recoverability threshold. |
| IPHE from public artifacts | `measurement_unidentifiable` | Endpoint/aggregate artifacts omit accepted and rejected candidate histories. |

The noncanonical `design_defeated` and `measurement_unidentifiable` values are
useful distinctions but violate SciAgent's documented three-value failure enum.
This repeats the Cycle-1 state-validation defect and must be resolved by a
schema decision, not by silently rewriting history.

### Reviewer history

- OMST consumed nine consecutive hypothesis-review dispatches, and every one
  returned `NEEDS_REVISION`. The mathematical claim improved, but successive
  defects migrated into executable correspondence, environment identity, and
  output authenticity.
- PQF consumed three additional dispatches: one `NEEDS_CONTEXT` with provisional
  adverse findings, its context-completion dispatch, and one v2 re-review. The
  final review still found a forced outcome and nondistinguishing gate stack.
- Cycle-2 hypothesis-review usage therefore rose from 11/20 at entry to 23/30:
  12 dispatches, no Phase-2 pass.

The repeated pattern is **reviewing specifications before proving that the
claim has an executable, independently grounded subject**.

### Budgets and gate behavior

- Research iterations ended at `5/5`; hypothesis reviews at `23/30`; paper
  reviews remained `2/4`.
- The OMST review loop was too permissive of repeated same-surface repair. After
  the second correspondence-related failure, an executable feasibility gate
  should have preceded any further review.
- PQF should have required an independently sourced advisory population before
  accepting an empirical effect-size hypothesis.
- The TBEA two-coder, label-firewall, coverage, concentration, and stop gates
  worked correctly and prevented a confounded association analysis.
- The IPHE novelty-plus-data audit worked correctly and conserved seven review
  rounds.

### User correction record

2. `dont ask me these questions again, go iterate and improve yourself`
   established an autonomy default but did not remove evidence gates.
3. `where are we now? ... preparing for writing academia before confirming

   was advancing while the competition result remained unconfirmed.
4. The current authorization makes the priority explicit: Kaggle actions are

The recurring correction pattern is **optimize the real objective and show its
