## Surface consistency test

The title, abstract, first contribution paragraph, Discussion opening, and final
sentence must all identify the same evidence unit: three named deterministic
crossed tables plus a homogeneous code-path sanity check. Any surface that uses
`material`, `opportunity`, `confirmatory`, `generalization`, `robustness`, or
`held-out` as a positive property of the executed result fails assembly unless
the term appears inside an explicit negation or historical quotation.
